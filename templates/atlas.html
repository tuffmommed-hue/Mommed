Good to know

How do I get there?

We send a map and arrange pickup from the nearest station on request.

Are meals included?

Breakfast and dinner are included. Lunch boxes can be packed for hikes.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes — two cabins are pet-friendly. Mention it when you book.

What's the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival.
